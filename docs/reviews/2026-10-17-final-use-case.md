(`judge-report.md:50-58`, `decisions.md:51-56`). That is the documented,

## What this proves / what it does not

**Proves**

- The pipeline runs end-to-end from a fresh CLI executor that did not build the
  skill: random seed (no `--seed`), 2 decision points, research on, card + points
  gate, 6 structural variants, judge loop, presentation, per-point pick, tokens /
  styleguide / handoff / chosen.
- The step-3 gate is real and observable: the first turn ends with `research.md`
  absent and only untouched variant shells on disk; generation starts after the
  relay.
- The post-cap rule survives contact: a known blocker fixed after 2 rounds is
  shipped with `NOT judge-verified` in both the report and `decisions.md`, not
  presented as judged.
- The mechanics are clean on the generated corpus: 20/20 HTML files pass
  `check_slop.py` individually (0 hits each), all 20 resolve their relative links,
  and `chosen/` is byte-identical to the winners.
- `file://` navigation works on this Chromium for gallery → canvas → variant →
  detail → back, including the per-record `?os=` fix and the d2-a panel.

**Does not prove**

- That the result passed the judge. `strangeness` failed twice and the final two
  majors were only author-verified; the artifact is honest about it, but the
  quality gate did not close.
- Visual quality or accessibility beyond one Chromium run. The audit browser
  reported a 1280×577 viewport; the judge's 390×844 mobile walkthrough was not
  independently repeated here. No Safari/iOS or screen-reader pass.
- That the research is complete or unbiased: six text-only sources (two candidates
  discarded), spot-checked but not re-read in full.
- Anything about the offline/font-fallback path; no offline run was made.
- Generalization across domains: one domain, one seed, one executor. The cut T25
  benchmark baseline is out of scope and was not required.
- That the executor's seed selection is ideal: drawing four seeds and keeping the
  first that passes `fits/fails` is defensible under the reroll rule, but the user
  never got the explicit "reroll is free" offer the skill asks for.

## Concerns (in priority order)

1. **No reroll offer** (item a). The card was shown correctly, but the free-reroll
   affordance was absent from the gate message; the user could only approve or
   edit points. This is a wording/step-2 gap, not a broken gate.
2. **Two majors shipped unverified.** The post-cap fix is correctly labelled, but
   the delivered prototypes still depend on author click-checks for the per-record
   detail and d2-a mobile behaviour. The label must travel into implementation.
3. **Canvas presentation at 1280.** Frames are 1280px in a padded scroll deck, so
   the right edge is clipped until the user scrolls horizontally; "abrir" opens a
   new tab. Functional, but the "side by side" comparison is weaker than it looks.
4. **Direction-DNA deviation.** 2–4px radii vs the D19's 6–10px; the judge logged
   it as a minor and it stayed open. Tokens faithfully mirror the chosen variants,
   which is the right call for handoff, but the card's DNA is not fully honored.
5. **Research depth.** Six sources at the cap, text-only; adequate for the
   decision points, not a competitive audit.
